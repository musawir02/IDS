01 · Who we are

Impulse Digital Solutions (IDS, “we”, “us”, or “our”) provides digital marketing, development, cybersecurity, creator, and business growth services. This policy describes our handling of information received through impulsedigitalgroup.com, website enquiries, our AI assistant, and lead forms associated with our advertising.

02 · Information we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, company, email address, phone number, location, service interests, and any details you choose to submit.
  • Meta lead forms: when you submit a Facebook or Instagram lead form for IDS, we receive the fields you submit, which may include prefilled contact details and your answers to enquiry questions.
  • Conversations: messages, correspondence, booking details, and information you provide during follow-up. Our AI assistant sends your message and the current conversation history to our server and Google Gemini to generate a response.
  • Technical information: our hosting and delivery providers may process IP addresses, browser and device information, requested pages, timestamps, and diagnostic or security logs. Contact form submissions are also recorded in server logs.

Please provide only information relevant to your enquiry. Do not submit sensitive personal information, passwords, or payment card details through forms or chat.

03 · How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, arrange consultations, prepare proposals, provide requested services, maintain business communications, operate and troubleshoot the website, prevent misuse, and meet applicable legal obligations.

If you submit a lead form, we use the contact details you provide to follow up about the service or offer described in that form. Submitting an enquiry does not by itself subscribe you to unrelated marketing. Where consent is required for promotional communications, we will seek it separately.

04 · Facebook & Instagram advertising

When you interact with our ads or submit a Meta Instant Form, Meta processes information under its own Privacy Policy. This policy describes how IDS handles information received from those forms. We use lead information for the purposes described in the form and this policy, including responding to your request.

The current website does not include a Meta Pixel or Conversions API integration. Visiting the website does not, through our application code, send pixel events or form details to Meta. Meta may separately measure interactions that take place on its platforms. If we introduce website advertising trackers, we will update this notice and provide any required consent or opt-out controls before activating them.

You can manage advertising preferences through your Meta account settings. To stop follow-up from IDS, contact us using the details below.

05 · Service providers & disclosures

Information may be processed by providers supporting website hosting (Hostinger), AI chat (Google Gemini), and communications or scheduling services you choose to use, such as Calendly. The information disclosed depends on the feature you use; website enquiries are not automatically sent to Gemini.

We may disclose information where required by law, to respond to lawful requests, to protect rights and security, or in connection with a business restructuring subject to applicable protections. External websites and platforms operate under their own privacy policies.

06 · Cookies & external resources

The website's current application code does not set advertising cookies or use browser storage to persist chat history. The main website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves requests to Google's servers and may disclose technical information such as your IP address.

Hosting infrastructure and external platforms you visit may use their own cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Blocking some technologies may affect functionality on external services.

07 · Retention & security

Retention depends on the purpose of collection, the status of an enquiry or client relationship, applicable recordkeeping obligations, and the retention settings of the providers involved. This includes contact submission logs and records held by external platforms. You can ask us about retention or request deletion using the contact details below.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please avoid including confidential project credentials or sensitive personal data in website forms and AI conversations.

08 · International processing

IDS has operations in the United States and the United Arab Emirates. Our providers may process information in these or other countries, whose privacy laws may differ from those where you live. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those requirements apply to our handling of your information.

09 · Your choices & privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; or opt out of certain advertising-related uses. You may also have the right to complain to your local privacy authority.

Send requests to grow@impulsedigitalsolutions.us with the subject “Privacy request”. Identify the email address or phone number used in your enquiry and describe your request. We may need to verify your identity and will respond as required by applicable law. Some records may need to be retained to meet legal obligations.

You can ask us to stop promotional contact at any time, or use an unsubscribe option where provided. This does not prevent necessary communications about services you have requested.

10 · Children's privacy

Our services and advertising enquiries are intended for business audiences, not children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address the request.

11 · Updates & contact

We may update this policy as our services and data practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Where required, we will provide additional notice of material changes.
